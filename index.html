Your afternoon at WOW

Heat

Start in the barrel sauna. Let the week melt off. Go back as many times as you want.

Cold

Then the plunge. It's a shock, it's a rush, and most people climb out grinning and go right back in. Athletes have used cold water to recover from hard training for decades, and early research links regular plunging to lower stress and better sleep. Our crew walks first-timers through it. Unlimited plunges.

Born in Lake Nona. Built for anyone who wants to feel better.

Not an expo not a gym

Central Florida is full of gyms, clinics, recovery studios and wellness brands, but they're scattered across town and you have to find each one yourself. WOW started in Lake Nona to bring them onto one lawn for one afternoon, so you can try everything, meet the people behind it, and leave knowing what works for you. Now we take that afternoon across Central Florida.

Mission

Make it easy and fun to build a healthier life.
